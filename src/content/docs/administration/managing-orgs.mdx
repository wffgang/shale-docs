When managning your organisations, user can invite members into the entire workspace. Member in the organisation will automatically have access to non-private project inside the workspace.

For each member in the organisation, role can be assigned according to responsiblity. 
Available options include "Admin", "Manager", "Creator" who have full access and "reviewer" and "viewer" with limited access.

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Pending conversation with Gareth |
| **Manager** | Pending conversation with Gareth |
| **Creator** | Pending conversation with Gareth |
| **Reviewer** | Pending conversation with Gareth |
| **Viewer** | Pending conversation with Gareth |

For any pending invites, organisation's owner can revoke their invites. For existing memebers, owner can remove them. 
![Organisation 2](/images/screenshots/Shale%20App%20Organisation%202.png)

For each project within the organisation, owner has additional control over who can access specific project.
